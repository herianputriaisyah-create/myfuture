Modern Infrastructure

World-Class Healthcare Technology

Designed with patient comfort, safety, and operational efficiency at its core.

Critical Care Unit

Next-Generation Intensive Care (ICU)

Our Intensive Care Unit features continuous telemetry, positive-pressure isolation rooms, and smart ventilator systems monitored 24/7 by board-certified intensivists.

  • 1:1 Nurse-to-Patient ratio for high-acuity care
  • HEPA filtration with HEPA-14 air exchange systems
  • Instant AI telemetry alert monitoring
Diagnostic Technology

Ultra-Silent 3T MRI & High-Speed CT

Equipped with ultra-wide bore MRI scanners designed to significantly reduce claustrophobia and produce high-definition neuro and musculoskeletal scans in half the standard scan time.

  • 70cm wide-bore scanner for maximum patient comfort
  • Low-dose radiation CT scan protocol
  • Same-day digital result portal access
Inpatient Care

Tranquil, Private Patient Suites

Engineered to mimic home-like comfort with ergonomic patient beds, companion sofa beds, climate control, and custom nutrition menus crafted by certified dietitians.

  • Private suite bathrooms with accessible walk-in showers
  • Smart tablet entertainment and nurse calling unit
  • Dedicated family lounge and dining options
Emergency Infrastructure

Rapid Triage & Emergency Trauma Hub

Direct ambulance bay integration with immediate bedside triage, integrated point-of-care lab testing, and dedicated emergency operating suites.

  • Zero-wait stroke and cardiac resuscitation suites
  • Rooftop helipad for urgent air ambulance transfers
  • On-site blood bank and emergency pharmacy
